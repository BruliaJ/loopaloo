The short version

Loopaloo does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no tracking and no analytics.

What stays on your device

To remember progress, Loopaloo saves stars, coins, unlocked pals, chosen outfits and a list of practiced words on the device itself. This information never leaves the device and is not sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app, or clearing the browser's site data on the web version, removes it.

Loopaloo never asks for a child's name. If a name is typed in to practice writing it, it is used only for that lesson and is not saved.

Microphone and speech

[Describe the voice feature for the version being submitted. Loopaloo itself never receives or stores audio. The microphone is only used when the button is pressed.]

Read-aloud voices use the device's built-in text-to-speech.

Children's privacy

Loopaloo is designed for children. Because we do not collect personal information from anyone, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (COPPA) or under 16 (GDPR).

Third parties

Loopaloo contains no third-party advertising, analytics or social features, and no links that take children out of the app.
